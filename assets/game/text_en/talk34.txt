#001//Prologue
Fleeing defeat, Kuchlug reached
Western Liao, caught the
princess's eye, and became royal
son-in-law overnight. Securing
troops from the Gurkhan, he
gathered Naiman remnants into a
formidable army. Conspiring with
Khwarazm, he staged a coup,
seized the Gurkhan, and declared
himself Emperor of Western Liao.
Kuchlug ruled with bloody
tyranny: he banned Islam, burned
crops, and unleashed his troops
to pillage and torment the folk.
In 1218, Genghis Khan appointed
Jebe as commander to crush
Kuchlug.
