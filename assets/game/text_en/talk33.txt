#001//Prologue
In 1213, Jin general Hushahu
staged a coup, murdered Wanyan
Yongji, and enthroned Wanyan Xun
as Xuanzong. Later, Zhuhu Gaoqi
slew Hushahu. Defeated by foes,
these commanders waged bloody
turmoil at court. After making
peace, the terrified court
resolved to flee to Kaifeng.
Only Yelu Chucai protested: 'If
the capital moves south, the
north cannot be held! Gather
grain and defend the capital.'
Yet Xuanzong fled south, leaving
Prince Wanyan Shouzhong, Wanyan
Fuxing, Moran Jinzhong, and Yelu
Chucai to hold Yanjing. When
Genghis Khan heard the Jin court
fled south, enraged at their bad
faith, he launched a second
southern campaign!
