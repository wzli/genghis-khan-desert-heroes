#001//Prologue
Temujin vanquished the Taichi'ut
and Tatars, taking their eastern
pastures. Ong Khan absorbed
Jamukha, while the old Kiyad
nobles Altan and Khuchar also
deserted to Ong Khan's court.
Across the vast steppe, only the
Mongols, Kereits, and Naimans
remained--a precarious balance
of three mighty powers. In 1203
AD, seeking to cement his ties
with Ong Khan, Temujin proposed
a double marriage alliance: his
eldest son Jochi would wed
Senggum's sister, uniting their
families as 'Quda' (in-laws).
Ong Khan welcomed the idea, and
after long hesitation, Senggum
nodded his agreement, inviting
Temujin to the betrothal
feast...
