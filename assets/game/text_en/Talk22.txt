#001//Prologue
The feud between Mongols and
Tatars stained the pages of
steppe history with blood. When
the Tatars submitted to the Jin
Dynasty, they acted as wolves
for the Jurchen masters, hunting
down Mongol heroes. Generation
after generation of Kiyad chiefs
poured their blood onto the
earth. Yet in a recent campaign,
the Tatars seized plundered
tribute for themselves, angering
the Jin court. The Jin sought an
alliance with the Mongols to
crush their unruly vassals.
Guided by the principle that
'the enemy of my enemy is my
friend', Temujin agreed to a
temporary pact with Jin. It was
a golden chance for the Mongols
to wash away generations of
blood vengeance.
