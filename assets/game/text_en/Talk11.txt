#001//Prologue
Across the northern steppe of
the Gobi, rival tribes stood
divided: the Mongols, Tatars,
Naimans, Kereits, and Merkits.
Pastures, herds, captives, and
iron became trophies of
ceaseless feuds. Blood vengeance
and tribal warfare raged across
the boundless plains.

In the autumn of 1162, a child
was born to Yesugei Baghatur,
chief of the Borjigin clan,
freshly triumphant over the
Tatars. Yesugei named his
newborn son 'Temujin', after his
captured rival, praying that he
would grow as resilient and
unyielding as tempered steel.

Raised amidst nomadic hunting,
Temujin enjoyed a spirited
childhood. This year, Temujin
turned eleven years old...
