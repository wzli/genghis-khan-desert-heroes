#001//Prologue
For four generations, feud
burned between Mongols and the
Jin Empire. When Khabul Khan
refused to bow at the Jin court,
Jin armies invaded the steppes,
yet suffered crushing defeat.
Jin then allied with Tatars
against the Mongols. Later,
Tatars captured Ambaghai Khan
and delivered him to Jin, who
nailed him upon a wooden donkey
in cruel execution. Every three
years, Jin troops marched north
to slaughter youths, calling it
'thinning the herd'-- countless
young men were murdered, and
women and children enslaved.
When Genghis Khan arose, he
feigned submission, but once the
steppes were united, war was
inevitable. In 1208, Emperor
Zhangzong died, and the inept
Wanyan Yongji took the throne.
Genghis Khan saw the hour for
vengeance had arrived: first
subdue Western Xia, then strike
the Jin Empire!
