#001//Prologue
In the spring of 1206 AD, along
the banks of the Onon River, the
Mongol clans erected the
Nine-Footed White Spirit Banner,
acclaiming Temujin as the Great
Khagan of all Mongolia. The
assembly bestowed the sacred
title 'Genghis Khan',
proclaiming the Great Mongol
State. Genghis Khan's first act
was the rewarding of his
meritorious generals, dividing
the realm into the Mingghan
System (Chiliarchs--commanders
of a thousand households), the
bedrock of Mongol rule.
