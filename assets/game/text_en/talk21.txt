#001//Prologue
Around Lake Hulun and Lake Buir
lies the lush Hulunbuir steppe.
The Nirun tribe dwelling here
was once part of the Mongols,
but had split away to live
independently. To safeguard
their power, they allied with
the Tatars--the ancient mortal
enemies of the Mongols.
Meanwhile, Buyruk Khan, brother
to Tayang Khan of the Naimans,
had broken away from his brother
and established his own
stronghold in the Altai
Mountains, constantly raiding
the Kereits. On the steppe, when
rival powers stand equally
matched, conflict reaches its
boiling point. To unite all
Mongols, Temujin set his sights
on reclaiming the Nirun. Yet
what consequences would this
conquest unleash?
