#001//Prologue
Following the death of Yesugei,
the Mongol people lost their
mighty and valiant leader. In
those days, succession was not
strictly hereditary; instead, a
Kurultai was held, where the
clans gathered to elect a Khan
of renown and stature. Among the
Mongol clans were the Taichi'ut,
Jurchin, Kiyad, and others, yet
none could put forth a leader of
supreme command. Thus, many
clansmen resolved to migrate
elsewhere.
