#001//Prologue
When the Jin drove the Song
south of the Yangtze, Jin ruled
the vast central plains while
Western Xia bowed as their
vassal. In 1206, Western Xia
Emperor Li Anquan took the
throne and secretly allied with
Jin against the Mongols. Feud
with Jin burned deep, yet to
strike Jin, Genghis Khan had
first to subdue Western Xia and
break the alliance. In 1209,
citing Li Anquan's refusal of
tribute, Genghis Khan launched a
grand campaign against Western
Xia.
